How to read the price ceiling

The ceiling is the most you can spend upfront on installed X-Mem per system and still hit your cost-per-token target at the entered throughput. A negative ceiling means the target is out of reach even if X-Mem is free.

Check fleet TCO separately: each fleet needs a whole number of systems. This is an undiscounted planning model. Validate throughput, fabric compatibility, and costs with the vendor.
